It seems easy to start a used-car business until you get to the licensing checklist. There are issues about zoning, business entities, bonds, insurance, fingerprints, signage, inspections, dealer education, tax registration, application forms and suddenly.

The good news is that the procedure is lot simpler when you execute the stages in the appropriate sequence.

An independent dealer licence usually permits a firm to acquire and sell used motor cars without having to operate under a manufacturer franchise. The precise name of the licence will vary from state to state. Texas employs the General Distinguishing Number, or GDN, system; Georgia licenses used motor vehicle dealers via a state board; California handles dealer licensing through DMV Occupational Licensing.

There is no independent national dealer licence. Your state determines the particular criteria.

## A better way to structure the application

One of the simplest ways to make the licensing process less stressful is to see it as a little project rather than just gathering paperwork haphazardly.

Create five folders in the following way:

1. Company Name

Keep your company creation, EIN confirmation, DBA, ownership documents and identity up to date here.

2. Place

Here, save the lease, zoning confirmation, certificate of occupancy, pictures of the property, sign information, and inspection notes.

3. FISCAL CONFORMITY

Keep the surety bond, power of attorney, insurance certificate, tax registration, and payment receipts close to hand.

4. Education and Background

This is where you save dealer course certificates, test results, fingerprint instructions, and any other background related documentation.

5. Applying and getting approval

Keep the final application, confirmation of submission, deficiency notes, emails, inspection result and licence approval here.

It seems easy enough, but a tidy folder organization helps avoid the typical dilemma of knowing that you “definitely have that document somewhere” as a license deadline looms.

Do not depend just on a landlord’s verbal response. The landlord may inform you that the area is commercially zoned, but the city might nevertheless tell you that your particular use is forbidden.

## When the State Sends a Deficiency Notice What to Do

A deficiency notice does not signify that you have failed your application.

Read it attentively and answer each item individually.

If the state requires 3 corrections, send 3 clearly marked corrections. Don’t submit a bunch of unrelated papers and trust that the reviewer will locate the solution.

For instance:

Deficiency 1: LLC does not match Bond name. Correct bond must be delivered.

## Deficiency 2 Sign picture is not legible. New high-res picture

Deficiency 3: Zoning form not signed. Complete the zoning form.

Make it simple for the reviewer. Clear apps tend to work better than complicated ones.
